### How the files were obtained

Audio was fetched from Freesound's publicly reachable preview CDN (`https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/...`, the `-hq.mp3` rendition), which requires no account. No accounts were created, nothing was signed into, and no terms-of-service dialog was accepted.

Processing rule applied with `ffmpeg`:

- source longer than 150 s -> trimmed to an 80 s section starting at 0:10 and re-encoded to 128 kbit/s stereo MP3 at 44.1 kHz;
- source shorter than 150 s but larger than ~1.5 MB -> re-encoded to 128 kbit/s stereo MP3, untrimmed;
- everything else copied byte-for-byte (`unmodified`).

Every file was checked with `ffprobe` after download and after processing: all decode as MP3 audio with a real duration, so no HTML error pages slipped through.

## Rejected / not used

| Source | Reason |
|---|---|
| **BBC Sound Effects Archive** (`sound-effects.bbcrewind.co.uk`) | The site's own text says sounds may be used "in your own personal/educational projects, or licence them for use" (BBC RemArc licence). That is personal/educational only, with commercial use behind a separate paid licence. **Rejected** under the commercial-use rule. |
| **radio aporee / Maps field recordings on archive.org** | Large, tempting collection of urban and subway field recordings, but item metadata is mostly `CC BY-NC-ND 3.0`. Verified directly via `https://archive.org/metadata/aporee_74260_86899`, which returns `licenseurl: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/3.0/`. **Rejected**: NonCommercial *and* NoDerivatives. |
| **Other archive.org audio items** | A significant share of `mediatype:audio` items return no `licenseurl` at all in their metadata, i.e. the licence is unstated. **Rejected** as unclear rather than assumed public domain. |
| **pixabay.com/sound-effects** | The Pixabay Content License would have been acceptable, but the site returned HTTP 403 to non-browser requests and its downloads are gated behind a signed-in session. Since creating accounts and signing in were out of scope, nothing was taken from Pixabay. **Not used** (access, not licence). |
| **Freesound sounds under non-CC0 licences** | Search was restricted to the `Creative Commons 0` licence facet throughout. CC BY 4.0 sounds would also have been acceptable, but CC0 candidates were plentiful, so attribution-encumbered and "Sampling+" licensed material was skipped entirely to keep the bundle unambiguous. |
| **SEO "free ambient sound" listicles / aggregator sites** | Not consulted as licence evidence. Every licence here comes from the hosting sound's own page. |

## Known gaps

- No dedicated **restaurant kitchen**, **rain on window** or **office keyboard** recording was added; those are either well covered by the synthesised voices or were not needed to reach good coverage.
- `church-interior` is a short (CC0) church room-tone recording titled "Church HH.wav"; the uploader gives no further description, so treat its room character as approximate.
- `pages-turning`, `pencil-writing`, `temple-bells` and `wind-chimes` are event recordings rather than seamless loops - they will need gap/crossfade handling in the mixer rather than naive looping.
